# Platform Overview

## What is #GangGreen?

#GangGreen is a comprehensive digital platform designed to catalyze a carbon-negative Africa by connecting stakeholders in environmental conservation, carbon credit markets, and sustainable development.

Built for Track 3 (Community Engagement and Sustainability) of the Wangari Maathai Hackathon, the platform facilitates tree planting initiatives, community engagement, and transparent monitoring of environmental impact across African regions.

---

## The Vision

**A carbon-negative Africa where every individual's climate action is recognized, rewarded, and verified.**

We believe that massive environmental change comes from millions of small actions. Like the hummingbird in the Kenyan folktale, every person doing their best creates collective impact that can transform our planet.

## How It Works

### The User Journey

```
Sign Up → Complete Challenges → Earn Rewards → Unlock Badges → Share Impact → Inspire Others
```

#### Step 1: Sign Up
Create your account in minutes. Choose your role (Individual, Organization, Community Member) and select your preferred pilot forest.

#### Step 2: Complete Challenges
Browse daily micro-challenges like:
- Plant a tree
- Share climate knowledge
- Join an initiative
- Reduce plastic use
- Educate others

#### Step 3: Earn Rewards
Every action earns GG Coins:
- Plant a tree: 10 GG Coins
- Join initiative: 5 GG Coins
- Share on social: 2 GG Coins
- Complete challenge: 3-15 GG Coins

#### Step 4: Unlock Badges
Collect NFT badges as you progress:
- **Bronze**: First steps (10 actions)
- **Silver**: Getting serious (50 actions)
- **Gold**: Climate champion (100 actions)
- **Platinum**: Environmental leader (500 actions)
- **Diamond**: Legendary impact (1000+ actions)

#### Step 5: Share Impact
Post your achievements on the social feed:
- Share photos of planted trees
- Celebrate milestones
- Inspire your network
- Build community

#### Step 6: Inspire Others
Your actions create ripple effects:
- Friends see your posts
- They join the movement
- You earn referral bonuses
- The community grows

- **Size**: 238 km²

- **Status**: Primary pilot site
