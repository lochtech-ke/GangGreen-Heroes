2. **Chat with Assistant** - Complete your profile conversationally (2 minutes)

## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Visit the Registration Page

1. Go to the #GangGreen platform
2. Click **"Sign Up"** or **"Get Started"**
3. You'll see a simple registration form

### Step 2: Enter Your Credentials

**Required Information**:
- **Email Address**: Your primary email
- **Password**: Minimum 8 characters
- **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password

**That's It!** No long forms to fill out.

### Step 3: Create Account

1. Review your information
2. Click **"Create Account"**
3. Wait for confirmation (usually instant)

**What Happens Next**:
- Your account is created immediately
- You're logged in automatically
- **The chatbot appears to help you complete your profile**

## Onboarding with the Chatbot

### What is the Onboarding Chatbot?

After registration, our friendly AI assistant will guide you through completing your profile in a conversational way. No forms, just chat!

**Status**: 🚧 Coming This Week (Backend Ready, UI In Development)

### How It Works

#### Step 1: Welcome Message

After registration, the chatbot automatically opens and greets you:

```
👋 Welcome to Gang Green, user@example.com!

I'm here to help you set up your profile. This will only take 
2-3 minutes. I'll ask you a few questions to personalize your 
experience.

Ready to get started? Let's begin! 

What's your full name?
```

#### Step 2: Answer Questions

The chatbot will ask you questions one at a time:

**Required Questions**:
1. **Full Name** - "What's your full name?"
2. **Role** - "How would you like to participate?"
   - Individual (support through donations)
   - Community Member (active local work)
   - Organization (manage initiatives)
3. **Forest Preference** - "Which forest would you like to focus on?"
   - Kakamega Forest
   - Karura Forest
   - Mau Forest
   - No preference

**Optional Questions** (you can skip):
4. **Phone Number** - "What's your phone number?" (Type "skip" to skip)
5. **Location** - "Where are you located?" (Type "skip" to skip)
6. **Organization** - "What's your organization name?" (Only if role = Organization)

#### Step 3: Confirmation

After answering all questions:

```
✅ Perfect! Your profile is complete.

Here's what you told me:
• Name: John Doe
• Role: Community Member
• Forest: Kakamega Forest
• Phone: +254712345678
• Location: Nairobi, Kenya

You can now explore the platform and join conservation initiatives!
```

### Tips for Onboarding

**Natural Conversation**:
- Answer in your own words
- The chatbot understands natural language
- No need for specific formats

**Skipping Questions**:
- Type "skip" to skip optional questions
- Type "I don't know" or "pass" also works
- Required questions cannot be skipped

**Correcting Mistakes**:
- If you make a mistake, just tell the chatbot
- Say "wait, I meant..." and provide the correct answer
- The chatbot will understand and update

**Getting Help**:
- Type "help" at any time
- Ask "what are my options?" for choices
- The chatbot will guide you

## FAQ

### Onboarding Chatbot

**Q: Do I have to use the chatbot?**  
A: Yes, the chatbot is the easiest way to complete your profile after registration. It only takes 2-3 minutes!

**Q: Can I skip questions?**  
A: You can skip optional questions (phone, location) by typing "skip". Required questions (name, role, forest) must be answered.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**  
A: Just tell the chatbot! Say "wait, I meant..." and provide the correct answer. You can also edit your profile later.

**Q: Can I use the chatbot after onboarding?**  
A: Yes! The chatbot is available anytime to answer questions about the platform.

**Q: What if the chatbot doesn't understand me?**  
A: Try rephrasing your question. If it still doesn't understand, it will offer to connect you with human support.

### Account & Registration

**Q: Why is registration so simple now?**  
A: We simplified registration to just email and password. The chatbot handles the rest conversationally, making it faster and more user-friendly.

**Q: Can I change my role later?**  
A: Yes! You can update your role in your profile settings or ask the chatbot to help you.

**Q: Is my information secure?**  
A: Yes! We use industry-standard encryption. Your data is stored securely and never shared without permission.
