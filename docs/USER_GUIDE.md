## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **Visit the Platform**: Navigate to the #GangGreen platform
2. **Click "Sign Up"**: Located in the top right corner
3. **Enter Your Details**:
   - Email address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Full name
   - Select your role (Individual, Community Member, Organization)
   - Choose your preferred forest (Kakamega, Karura, or Mau)
4. **Verify Your Email**: Check your inbox for a verification link
5. **Complete Your Profile**: Add a profile picture and bio

### Logging In

1. Click "Login" in the top right corner
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"

### Forgot Password?

1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for a reset link
4. Create a new password

## Tree Planting Initiatives

### Browsing Initiatives

1. Click "Initiatives" in the main navigation
2. Use filters to find initiatives:
   - **Forest**: Kakamega, Karura, or Mau
   - **Status**: Active, Planning, Completed
   - **Sort by**: Newest, Most Popular, Ending Soon

### Viewing Initiative Details

Click on any initiative card to see:
- Full description and goals
- Location on interactive map
- Progress towards tree planting target
- List of participants
- Organization information
- Timeline and milestones

### Joining an Initiative

1. Open the initiative details page
2. Click "Join Initiative"
3. Confirm your participation
4. You'll receive notifications about updates and milestones

### Creating an Initiative (Organizations Only)

1. Click "Create Initiative" from the Initiatives page
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Title**: Clear, descriptive name
   - **Description**: Goals, methods, and expected impact
   - **Forest**: Select Kakamega, Karura, or Mau
   - **Target Trees**: Number of trees to plant
   - **Target Area**: Hectares to cover
   - **Location**: Pin on map or enter coordinates
   - **Timeline**: Start and end dates
3. Upload supporting documents (optional)
4. Click "Create Initiative"
5. Your initiative will be reviewed and published

### Managing Your Initiative

1. Go to "My Initiatives" in your dashboard
2. Click on an initiative to manage it
3. Available actions:
   - Update progress
   - Add milestone updates
   - Upload photos
   - Manage participants
   - Generate reports

## Tree Registry & Monitoring

### Registering a Tree

1. Click "Register Tree" from the Trees page
2. Enter tree details:
   - **Species**: Select from dropdown or enter custom
   - **Planted Date**: When the tree was planted
   - **Location**: Pin on map or enter coordinates
   - **Forest**: Kakamega, Karura, or Mau
   - **Initiative**: Link to an initiative (optional)
3. Upload initial photo
4. Click "Register Tree"

### Uploading Tree Photos

1. Go to the tree's detail page
2. Click "Upload Photo"
3. Select image from your device
4. Add capture date and notes
5. Click "Upload"

**Photo Guidelines**:
- Clear, well-lit images
- Show full tree or specific features
- Maximum file size: 10MB
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG, HEIC

### AI-Powered Monitoring

Trees registered on the platform are automatically monitored using Antugrow AI:

- **Health Analysis**: AI detects diseases, pests, and stress
- **Growth Tracking**: Automatic height and diameter measurements
- **Recommendations**: Personalized care suggestions
- **Alerts**: Notifications when attention is needed

### Viewing Tree Health Status

1. Open any tree's detail page
2. View the health status indicator:
   - 🟢 **Healthy**: Tree is thriving
   - 🟡 **Needs Attention**: Minor issues detected
   - 🔴 **Critical**: Immediate action required
   - ⚫ **Deceased**: Tree has died
3. Click "View Analysis" for detailed AI insights

### Tree Growth Charts

Track your tree's growth over time:
- Height progression graph
- Diameter measurements
- Photo timeline
- Health score history

## Carbon Credit Marketplace

### Understanding Carbon Credits

Carbon credits represent the amount of CO₂ sequestered by trees. One credit = one tonne of CO₂ removed from the atmosphere.

**Verification Process**:
- Credits are generated from verified initiatives
- Third-party verification ensures accuracy
- Blockchain tracking for transparency

### Browsing Available Credits

1. Click "Marketplace" in the main navigation
2. View available carbon credits
3. Filter by:
   - Forest location
   - Price range
   - Verification status
   - Vintage year

### Purchasing Carbon Credits

1. Click on a credit listing
2. Review details:
   - Initiative information
   - Verification status
   - Price per tonne
   - Available quantity
3. Enter quantity to purchase
4. Review total price
5. Select payment method:
   - Credit/Debit Card
   - Cryptocurrency (ETH, MATIC, USDC)
6. Complete payment
7. Receive digital certificate

### Your Carbon Credit Portfolio

1. Go to "My Credits" in your dashboard
2. View all purchased credits
3. Download certificates
4. Track environmental impact
5. Share achievements on social media

### Transaction History

1. Click "Transactions" in your dashboard
2. View all purchases and donations
3. Filter by date, type, or status
4. Download receipts
5. Request refunds (if eligible)

## NFT Badges

### What are NFT Badges?

NFT badges are unique digital collectibles that recognize your conservation achievements. They're stored on the blockchain and truly owned by you.

**Badge Tiers**:
- 🥉 **Bronze**: Entry-level achievements
- 🥈 **Silver**: Significant contributions
- 🥇 **Gold**: Major milestones
- 💎 **Platinum**: Exceptional impact

### Earning Badges

Badges are earned by:
- Planting trees
- Making donations
- Completing challenges
- Reaching milestones
- Referring friends
- Consistent participation

### Badge Types

1. **Tree Planter**: Plant 10, 50, 100, or 500 trees
2. **Carbon Warrior**: Offset 1, 10, 50, or 100 tonnes CO₂
3. **Crypto Donor**: Donate $100, $500, $1000, or $5000
4. **Community Leader**: Recruit 5, 20, 50, or 100 members
5. **Forest Guardian**: Complete forest-specific challenges
6. **Quest Master**: Complete 5, 10, 25, or 50 quests

### Minting Your Badge

1. When you earn a badge, you'll receive a notification
2. Click "Mint Badge" in the notification
3. Connect your Web3 wallet (if not connected)
4. Review badge details and rarity
5. Click "Mint NFT"
6. Approve transaction in your wallet
7. Wait for minting confirmation
8. Badge appears in your gallery!

### Badge Gallery

1. Click "My Badges" in your profile
2. View all earned and minted badges
3. Filter by type, tier, or date
4. Click a badge to see details:
   - Rarity and supply
   - Earning criteria
   - Mint date and transaction
   - Blockchain verification link

### Sharing Your Badges

1. Open badge details
2. Click "Share"
3. Choose platform:
   - Twitter
   - Facebook
   - LinkedIn
   - Copy link
4. Show off your impact!

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is #GangGreen free to use?**
A: Yes! Creating an account and participating in initiatives is completely free.

**Q: Which forests can I support?**
A: Currently, we focus on three Kenyan forests: Kakamega, Karura, and Mau.

**Q: How are trees verified?**
A: Trees are monitored using AI-powered analysis through Antugrow API and verified by local organizations.

### Carbon Credits

**Q: How are carbon credits calculated?**
A: Credits are calculated based on tree species, age, and growth data, verified by third-party auditors.

**Q: Can I resell carbon credits?**
A: Currently, credits are for personal offset only. Resale features coming soon.

**Q: Are carbon credits tax-deductible?**
A: Consult your tax advisor. We provide receipts for your records.

### Web3 & Crypto

**Q: Do I need a crypto wallet?**
A: Only if you want to make crypto donations or mint NFT badges. Traditional payments are also available.

**Q: Which cryptocurrencies are accepted?**
A: ETH, MATIC, and USDC on Ethereum and Polygon networks.

**Q: Are NFT badges valuable?**
A: Badges are primarily recognition of your impact, not financial investments.

### Technical Issues

**Q: I can't upload photos. What should I do?**
A: Ensure images are under 10MB and in JPG/PNG format. Try a different browser if issues persist.

**Q: My wallet won't connect. Help!**
A: Make sure you're on a supported network (Ethereum or Polygon) and your wallet is unlocked.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Contact support at support@ganggreen.org to request account deletion.

## Glossary

**Carbon Credit**: Certificate representing 1 tonne of CO₂ removed from atmosphere

**Initiative**: Organized tree planting project in a specific forest

**NFT Badge**: Blockchain-based digital collectible recognizing achievements

**RLS**: Row Level Security - database security ensuring data privacy

**Supabase**: Backend platform powering the application

**Web3**: Decentralized internet technologies including blockchain and crypto

**Antugrow**: AI-powered tree monitoring and analysis service

**MATIC**: Cryptocurrency native to Polygon network

**Gas Fee**: Transaction fee paid to blockchain network

**Minting**: Creating a new NFT on the blockchain
