## Getting Started

### What You'll Need

- Email address
- Password (minimum 8 characters)
- 2-3 minutes to complete your profile

### Quick Start Steps

1. **Register** - Create your account with email and password (30 seconds)
2. **Complete Profile** - Add your details and preferences (2 minutes)
3. **Explore** - Start participating in conservation efforts

## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Visit the Registration Page

1. Go to the #GangGreen platform
2. Click **"Sign Up"** or **"Get Started"**
3. You'll see a simple registration form

### Step 2: Enter Your Credentials

**Required Information**:
- **Email Address**: Your primary email
- **Password**: Minimum 8 characters
- **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password

**Tips**:
- Use a strong, unique password
- Make sure you have access to your email
- Password must be at least 8 characters

### Step 3: Create Account

1. Review your information
2. Click **"Create Account"**
3. Wait for confirmation (usually instant)

**What Happens Next**:
- Your account is created immediately
- You're logged in automatically
- You'll be prompted to complete your profile

## Completing Your Profile

### Profile Information

After registration, complete your profile with:

#### 1. Your Full Name
- Required for personalization
- Used in your public profile

#### 2. How You Want to Participate
Choose your role:
- 🌱 **Individual** - Support through donations and participation
- 🤝 **Community Member** - Active local conservation work
- 🏢 **Organization** - Manage initiatives and projects

#### 3. Organization Name (If Applicable)
- Only required if you selected "Organization"
- Your organization's official name

#### 4. Forest Preference (Optional)
Choose which forest to focus on:
- 🌳 **Kakamega Forest** - Primary pilot site
- 🌲 **Karura Forest** - Urban conservation area
- 🌴 **Mau Forest** - Critical water tower ecosystem
- ⏭️ **No preference** - Support all forests

#### 5. Additional Information (Optional)
- Phone number
- Location
- Profile picture

## Tree Planting Initiatives

### What Are Initiatives?

Tree planting initiatives are organized conservation projects where organizations coordinate efforts to plant trees, restore forests, and sequester carbon.

**Status**: 🚧 Coming Soon (Backend Ready, UI In Development)

### How Initiatives Work

#### For Organizations

**Create an Initiative**:
1. Go to "Create Initiative" (coming soon)
2. Enter initiative details:
   - Title and description
   - Target number of trees
   - Start and end dates
   - Location on map
   - Area in hectares
   - Forest selection
3. Submit for review
4. Manage participants and track progress

**Example Initiative**:
```
Title: Kakamega Forest Restoration 2025
Description: Community-led initiative to plant 10,000 indigenous trees
Forest: Kakamega
Target: 10,000 trees
Duration: January 1 - December 31, 2025
Location: 34.8522°E, 0.2827°N
Area: 50 hectares
```

#### For Participants

**Join an Initiative**:
1. Browse active initiatives (coming soon)
2. Filter by forest or search by name
3. View initiative details and progress
4. Click "Join Initiative"
5. Start contributing trees

**Track Your Contributions**:
- See how many trees you've contributed
- View initiative progress
- Check if the initiative is on track
- Receive milestone notifications

### Initiative Features (Coming Soon)

**Browse & Search**:
- View all active initiatives
- Filter by forest (Kakamega, Karura, Mau)
- Search by title or description
- See initiative status (active, completed, paused)

**Initiative Details**:
- Title and description
- Target vs. planted trees
- Progress percentage
- Days remaining
- Location on interactive map
- Participant count
- Organization information

**Progress Tracking**:
- Real-time progress updates
- Trees planted vs. target
- Days remaining
- On-track indicator (green/yellow/red)
- Participant contributions

**Geospatial Features**:
- View initiatives on map
- See forest boundaries
- Filter by location
- Visualize conservation areas

### Example User Journey

**As a Community Member**:
1. Log in to your dashboard
2. Click "Browse Initiatives"
3. Filter by "Kakamega Forest"
4. Find "Kakamega Restoration 2025"
5. View details and progress (6,500/10,000 trees)
6. Click "Join Initiative"
7. Register trees you plant
8. Update your contribution (e.g., 50 trees)
9. Track initiative progress
10. Receive notification when target is reached

**As an Organization**:
1. Log in to your dashboard
2. Click "Create Initiative"
3. Fill in initiative details
4. Set location on map
5. Submit initiative
6. Invite community members
7. Monitor participant contributions
8. Update trees planted
9. Track progress toward goal
10. Mark as completed when target reached

## FAQ

### Account & Registration

**Q: Why do I need to complete my profile?**  
A: Your profile helps us personalize your experience and connect you with relevant conservation opportunities. Some features require a complete profile.

**Q: Can I change my role later?**  
A: Yes! You can update your role in your profile settings at any time.

**Q: Is my information secure?**  
A: Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and security practices. Your data is stored securely and never shared without your permission.

### Initiatives

**Q: When will I be able to join initiatives?**  
A: The initiative management system is currently in development. The backend is complete, and we're building the user interface now. Expected launch: Next week!

**Q: Can I create an initiative as an individual?**  
A: No, only organizations can create initiatives. However, individuals and community members can join and contribute to existing initiatives.

**Q: How is progress tracked?**  
A: Progress is calculated based on trees planted vs. target trees. The system also tracks if an initiative is on track based on time elapsed and progress made.

**Q: What happens when an initiative reaches its goal?**  
A: The initiative is marked as "completed" and participants receive achievement notifications. The organization can then create a new initiative or extend the existing one.

### Profile Management

**Q: Can I delete my account?**  
A: Yes. Contact support@ganggreen.org to request account deletion. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

**Q: How do I change my forest preference?**  
A: Go to "Edit Profile" and select a different forest from the dropdown menu.

**Q: What if I represent multiple organizations?**  
A: Currently, each account can be associated with one organization. Contact support if you need to manage multiple organizations.

### Technical Issues

**Q: I didn't receive a verification email**  
A: Check your spam folder. If still not found, click "Resend verification email" on the login page.

**Q: I forgot my password**  
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions to reset it.
