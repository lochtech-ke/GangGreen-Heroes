## Carbon Credit Marketplace

### What Are Carbon Credits?

Carbon credits represent verified carbon sequestration from tree planting initiatives. Each credit equals one ton of CO2 removed from the atmosphere.

**Status**: ✅ FULLY OPERATIONAL

## Purchasing Carbon Credits

### Step-by-Step Purchase Guide

**Prerequisites**:
- You must be logged in
- Credit must be verified
- Sufficient quantity must be available

**Steps**:

1. **Select a Credit**
   - Browse the marketplace
   - Click on a credit card
   - Review credit details

2. **Click "Purchase"**
   - Purchase button on credit card or details page
   - Purchase modal opens

3. **Enter Quantity**
   - Specify how many tons you want to purchase
   - See real-time price calculation
   - View price in both USD and KES
   - Check availability

4. **Review Purchase**
   - Verify quantity and total amount
   - Review payment currency (KES)
   - Check initiative details
   - Click "Proceed to Payment"

5. **Complete Payment**
   - Paystack payment popup opens
   - Choose payment method:
     - **Card**: Enter card details
     - **Bank Transfer**: Select bank and authorize
     - **M-Pesa**: Enter phone number
   - Complete payment securely
   - Wait for verification (usually instant)

6. **Confirmation**
   - Success screen displays
   - Transaction details shown
   - Certificate download link provided
   - Option to view transaction history

### Payment Methods

#### Credit/Debit Cards

**Supported Cards**:
- Visa
- Mastercard
- Verve

**Process**:
1. Select "Pay with Card"
2. Enter card number, expiry, CVV
3. Enter billing address
4. Click "Pay"
5. Complete 3D Secure verification (if required)
6. Receive instant confirmation

#### Bank Transfer

**Supported Banks**:
- All major Nigerian and Kenyan banks
- Real-time bank transfer
- Instant verification

**Process**:
1. Select "Pay with Bank Transfer"
2. Choose your bank from the list
3. Authorize payment in your banking app
4. Return to platform
5. Receive confirmation

#### M-Pesa (Kenya)

**Requirements**:
- Kenyan M-Pesa account
- Sufficient M-Pesa balance

**Process**:
1. Select "Pay with M-Pesa"
2. Enter your M-Pesa phone number
3. Receive STK push on your phone
4. Enter M-Pesa PIN
5. Receive instant confirmation

### Currency and Pricing

**Display Currency**: USD (primary)
**Payment Currency**: KES (Kenyan Shillings)

**Exchange Rate**: 1 USD = 150 KES (configurable)

**Example**:
- Credit price: $25.00 per ton
- Quantity: 10 tons
- Total USD: $250.00
- Total KES: KES 37,500.00
- You pay: KES 37,500.00

### Transaction Confirmation

After successful payment, you'll receive:

1. **On-Screen Confirmation**
   - Transaction ID
   - Amount paid
   - Quantity purchased
   - Payment method used
   - Date and time

2. **Email Receipt** (coming soon)
   - Detailed transaction information
   - Certificate attachment

3. **Certificate**
   - Downloadable PDF

### Transaction History

View all your purchases:

1. Go to your profile

**Transaction Statuses**:

## FAQ

### Payment Questions

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**  
A: We accept credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve), bank transfers, and M-Pesa mobile money.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**  
A: Yes! All payments are processed by Paystack, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. We never store your card details.

**Q: Why do I pay in KES when prices are in USD?**  
A: Paystack processes payments in local currency (KES) for better rates and faster processing. The conversion rate is clearly displayed before payment.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**  
A: Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. Contact support@ganggreen.org with your transaction reference.

**Q: How long does payment verification take?**  
A: Most payments are verified instantly. Bank transfers may take a few minutes. If verification takes longer than 10 minutes, contact support.

**Q: Can I purchase credits for someone else?**  
A: Yes! You can purchase credits and gift them to others. Contact support for assistance with gift purchases.

**Q: What happens if my payment fails?**  
A: If payment fails, no charge is made and the carbon credits remain available. You can try again with a different payment method.

**Q: Do you accept international payments?**  
A: Currently, we primarily support Kenyan payment methods. International card payments may be supported depending on your card issuer.

### Carbon Credit Questions

**Q: What is a carbon credit?**  
A: A carbon credit represents one ton of CO2 removed from the atmosphere through tree planting and verified by our system.

**Q: Are the carbon credits verified?**  
A: Yes! All credits in the marketplace are verified through our rigorous verification process and linked to real tree planting initiatives.

**Q: Can I resell carbon credits?**  
A: Currently, carbon credits are for personal or organizational carbon offsetting only. Resale functionality may be added in the future.

**Q: How do I use my carbon credits?**  
A: Carbon credits offset your carbon footprint. You'll receive a certificate that you can use for reporting, marketing, or personal satisfaction.

**Q: Do carbon credits expire?**  
A: No, carbon credits do not expire. The carbon sequestration is permanent as long as the trees remain healthy.
