## Tree Registry & Monitoring

### What is the Tree Registry?

The tree registry is a comprehensive system for tracking individual trees you've planted, monitoring their growth, and receiving AI-powered insights about their health and development.

**Status**: ✅ FULLY OPERATIONAL

### Registering a Tree

#### Step 1: Navigate to Tree Registry

1. Go to your dashboard
2. Click **"Register a Tree"** or navigate to **"My Trees"**
3. Click the **"Add New Tree"** button

#### Step 2: Enter Tree Information

**Required Information**:
- **Species**: Select from common species or enter custom
  - Examples: Acacia, Eucalyptus, Mango, Avocado
  - Indigenous species are highlighted
- **Planting Date**: When the tree was planted
- **Location**: Click on map or enter coordinates
  - Use preset buttons for quick forest selection
  - Or click anywhere on the map

**Optional Information**:
- **Initiative**: Associate with an initiative (if applicable)
- **Current Height**: Initial height measurement (in meters)
- **Notes**: Any additional information about the tree

#### Step 3: Upload Initial Photo (Optional)

- Drag and drop a photo or click to browse
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP
- Maximum size: 10MB
- Photo will be automatically analyzed by AI

#### Step 4: Submit

1. Review your information
2. Click **"Register Tree"**
3. Wait for confirmation
4. Your tree is now registered!

**What Happens Next**:
- Tree is registered in the system
- If you uploaded a photo, AI analysis begins automatically
- You'll receive a notification when analysis is complete
- Tree appears in your tree registry

### Uploading Monitoring Photos

#### Why Upload Photos?

Regular photos help:
- Track tree growth over time
- Monitor tree health
- Receive AI-powered insights
- Get personalized recommendations
- Document your conservation impact

**Recommended Frequency**: Monthly or quarterly

#### How to Upload

1. Go to tree details page
2. Click **"Upload Photo"** button
3. Select or drag-and-drop image
4. Add optional notes about the photo
5. Click **"Upload and Analyze"**

**Photo Guidelines**:
- Take photos in good lighting
- Include full tree in frame
- Take from consistent angle
- Avoid obstructions
- Include reference objects for scale

**What Happens Next**:
- Photo is uploaded to secure storage
- AI analysis begins automatically (takes 1-2 minutes)
- You'll receive a notification when complete
- Analysis results appear in tree details

### Understanding AI Analysis

#### Growth Predictions

**What You'll See**:
- Predicted height over next 6-12 months
- Growth rate (cm/month)
- Confidence level (percentage)
- Comparison to typical growth for species

**Example**:
```
Current Height: 2.5 meters
Predicted Height (6 months): 3.2 meters
Growth Rate: 12 cm/month
Confidence: 85%
```

#### Health Assessment

**Health Status Indicators**:
- 🟢 **Healthy**: Tree is thriving, no issues detected
- 🟡 **Needs Attention**: Minor issues detected, monitoring recommended
- 🔴 **Critical**: Serious issues detected, immediate action needed
- ⚪ **Unknown**: Insufficient data for assessment

**Health Factors Analyzed**:
- Leaf color and density
- Branch structure
- Trunk condition
- Signs of disease or pests
- Environmental stress indicators

#### Personalized Recommendations

Based on AI analysis, you'll receive:
- **Watering recommendations**: Frequency and amount
- **Fertilization advice**: Type and timing
- **Pest management**: If issues detected
- **Pruning guidance**: When and how to prune
- **Environmental tips**: Shade, wind protection, etc.

**Example Recommendations**:
```
1. Increase watering frequency to twice weekly
2. Apply nitrogen-rich fertilizer in next 2 weeks
3. Monitor for signs of leaf rust disease
4. Consider adding mulch around base
5. Prune lower branches for better air circulation
```

### Tree Registry & Monitoring (NEW!)

**Q: How do I register a tree?**  
A: Navigate to "My Trees" and click "Add New Tree". Fill in the species, planting date, and location. Optionally upload a photo for immediate AI analysis.

**Q: Can I register trees I planted before joining the platform?**  
A: Yes! You can register any tree you've planted, regardless of when. Just enter the correct planting date.

**Q: How often should I upload monitoring photos?**  
A: We recommend monthly or quarterly photos for best results. More frequent photos provide better growth tracking and health monitoring.

**Q: What happens to my photos?**  
A: Photos are securely stored and analyzed by our AI partner, Antugrow. They're used to generate growth predictions and health assessments. Only you and your initiative organizers (if applicable) can see your photos.

**Q: How accurate are the AI predictions?**  
A: AI predictions typically have 80-90% confidence levels. Accuracy improves with more photos and data over time. Predictions are based on species characteristics, environmental conditions, and historical data.

**Q: What if the AI detects a problem with my tree?**  
A: You'll receive a notification with specific recommendations. The health status will change to "Needs Attention" or "Critical" depending on severity. Follow the recommendations provided, and consider consulting a local forestry expert for serious issues.

**Q: Can I delete a tree from the registry?**  
A: Yes, you can delete trees you've registered. However, this action is permanent and cannot be undone. All associated photos and analysis data will also be deleted.

**Q: How does the background sync work?**  
A: The system automatically checks for new data from Antugrow every 2 hours. This ensures your growth predictions and health assessments are always up-to-date without requiring manual action.

**Q: Can I export my tree data?**  
A: Export functionality is coming soon. You'll be able to download your tree data, photos, and analysis results in CSV and PDF formats.

**Q: What tree species are supported?**  
A: The AI supports hundreds of tree species, with a focus on indigenous African species. If your species isn't in the dropdown, you can enter it manually. The AI will still provide analysis based on similar species characteristics.

## Quick Reference

### Tree Health Status Colors

- 🟢 **Green (Healthy)**: Tree is thriving, no issues detected
- 🟡 **Yellow (Needs Attention)**: Minor issues, monitoring recommended
- 🔴 **Red (Critical)**: Serious issues, immediate action needed
- ⚪ **Gray (Unknown)**: Insufficient data for assessment

### Photo Upload Guidelines

- **Frequency**: Monthly or quarterly
- **Lighting**: Good natural light, avoid harsh shadows
- **Framing**: Include full tree in frame
- **Angle**: Consistent angle for comparison
- **Size**: Maximum 10MB
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP

### Common Tree Species

**Indigenous Species** (recommended):
- Acacia (various species)
- Croton megalocarpus
- Markhamia lutea
- Prunus africana
- Warburgia ugandensis

**Exotic Species**:
- Eucalyptus
- Grevillea robusta
- Cypress
- Pine
