## Getting Started

### What You'll Need

- Email address
- Password (minimum 8 characters)
- 2-3 minutes to complete your profile

### Quick Start Steps

1. **Register** - Create your account with email and password (30 seconds)
2. **Complete Profile** - Chat with our onboarding assistant (2 minutes)
3. **Explore** - Start participating in conservation efforts

## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Visit the Registration Page

1. Go to the #GangGreen platform
2. Click **"Sign Up"** or **"Get Started"**
3. You'll see a simple registration form

### Step 2: Enter Your Credentials

**Required Information**:
- **Email Address**: Your primary email
- **Password**: Minimum 8 characters
- **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password

**Tips**:
- Use a strong, unique password
- Make sure you have access to your email
- Password must be at least 8 characters

### Step 3: Create Account

1. Review your information
2. Click **"Create Account"**
3. Wait for confirmation (usually instant)

**What Happens Next**:
- Your account is created immediately
- You're logged in automatically
- The onboarding assistant appears to help you complete your profile

## Completing Your Profile

### Meet Your Onboarding Assistant 🤖

After registration, you'll be greeted by our friendly onboarding chatbot who will help you complete your profile through a simple conversation.

### What the Chatbot Will Ask

#### 1. Your Full Name
```
Bot: "Welcome to #GangGreen! Let's complete your profile. 
      What's your full name?"

You: "John Doe"

Bot: "Nice to meet you, John!"
```

#### 2. How You Want to Participate
```
Bot: "How would you like to participate in conservation efforts?"

Options:
- 🌱 Individual - Support through donations and participation
- 🤝 Community Member - Active local conservation work
- 🏢 Organization - Manage initiatives and projects
```

**Choose the role that best fits you**:
- **Individual**: Perfect for supporters and donors
- **Community Member**: For active local participants
- **Organization**: For NGOs, companies, and groups

#### 3. Organization Name (If Applicable)
```
[Only if you selected "Organization"]

Bot: "What's your organization's name?"

You: "Green Earth NGO"
```

#### 4. Forest Preference (Optional)
```
Bot: "Which forest would you like to focus on?"

Options:
- 🌳 Kakamega Forest - Primary pilot site
- 🌲 Karura Forest - Urban conservation area
- 🌴 Mau Forest - Critical water tower ecosystem
- ⏭️ No preference - I want to support all forests
```

#### 5. Additional Information (Optional)
```
Bot: "Would you like to add your phone number and location? 
      (You can skip this and add it later)"

Options:
- Add phone and location
- Skip for now
```

### Completing the Process

```
Bot: "Perfect! Your profile is complete. Let's get started!"

[You're automatically redirected to your dashboard]
```

### Skip and Complete Later

**Not ready to complete your profile?**
- Click **"Skip for now"** at any time
- You can complete your profile later from your dashboard
- Some features may be limited until your profile is complete

## Profile Management

### Viewing Your Profile

1. Click your profile picture in the top right
2. Select **"View Profile"**
3. See all your profile information

### Editing Your Profile

1. Go to your profile page
2. Click **"Edit Profile"**
3. Update any information:
   - Full name
   - Role
   - Forest preference
   - Organization
   - Phone number
   - Location
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Uploading a Profile Picture

1. Go to **"Edit Profile"**
2. Click on your current avatar or **"Upload Photo"**
3. Select an image from your device
4. Crop if needed
5. Click **"Save"**

**Photo Requirements**:
- Maximum size: 2MB
- Formats: JPG, PNG, WebP
- Recommended: Square image, at least 200x200 pixels

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to **"Profile Settings"**
2. Click **"Security"** tab
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter your new password
5. Confirm new password
6. Click **"Update Password"**

### Changing Your Email

1. Go to **"Profile Settings"**
2. Click **"Account"** tab
3. Enter your new email address
4. Verify with your current password
5. Check your new email for verification link
6. Click the link to confirm

## FAQ

### Account & Registration

**Q: Why do I need to complete my profile?**  
A: Your profile helps us personalize your experience and connect you with relevant conservation opportunities. Some features require a complete profile.

**Q: Can I change my role later?**  
A: Yes! You can update your role in your profile settings at any time.

**Q: What if I skip the onboarding chatbot?**  
A: No problem! You can complete your profile later from your dashboard. However, some features may be limited until your profile is complete.

**Q: Is my information secure?**  
A: Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and security practices. Your data is stored securely and never shared without your permission.

### Profile Management

**Q: Can I delete my account?**  
A: Yes. Contact support@ganggreen.org to request account deletion. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

**Q: How do I change my forest preference?**  
A: Go to "Edit Profile" and select a different forest from the dropdown menu.

**Q: What if I represent multiple organizations?**  
A: Currently, each account can be associated with one organization. Contact support if you need to manage multiple organizations.

### Technical Issues

**Q: I didn't receive a verification email**  
A: Check your spam folder. If still not found, click "Resend verification email" on the login page.

**Q: I forgot my password**  
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions to reset it.

**Q: The onboarding chatbot isn't working**  
A: Try refreshing the page. If the issue persists, you can skip the chatbot and complete your profile manually from your dashboard.

### Getting Started

**Q: What should I do after creating my account?**  
A: Complete your profile with the onboarding chatbot, then explore your dashboard. Check out active initiatives and learn about the three pilot forests.

**Q: Do I need to choose a forest preference?**  
A: No, it's optional! You can support all three forests or focus on one that's meaningful to you.

**Q: Can I participate if I'm not in Kenya?**  
A: Absolutely! #GangGreen welcomes global participation. You can support conservation efforts from anywhere in the world.
