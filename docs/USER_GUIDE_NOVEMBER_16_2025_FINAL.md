1. Review your information
2. Click **"Create Account"**

- Your account is created immediately
- You're logged in automatically
- You'll be prompted to complete your profile

## Tree Planting Initiatives

### What Are Initiatives?

Tree planting initiatives are organized conservation projects where organizations coordinate efforts to plant trees, restore forests, and sequester carbon.

**Status**: ✅ FULLY OPERATIONAL

### Browsing Initiatives

#### List View

1. Navigate to "Initiatives" from the main menu
2. See all active initiatives in a grid layout
3. Each card shows:
   - Initiative title and description
   - Forest location (Kakamega, Karura, or Mau)
   - Status (Active, Completed, Paused)
   - Progress bar with percentage
   - Trees planted vs. target
   - Area in hectares
   - Timeline (start and end dates)

#### Filtering Initiatives

Use the filter panel to find specific initiatives:

**Forest Filter**:
- All Forests
- Kakamega Forest
- Karura Forest
- Mau Forest

**Status Filter**:
- All Statuses
- Active
- Completed
- Paused

**Search**:
- Search by title or description
- Real-time filtering as you type

#### Map View

1. Click "View on Map" to see initiatives geographically
2. Interactive map shows all initiatives as markers
3. Markers are color-coded by status:
   - 🟢 Green: Active initiatives
   - 🔵 Blue: Completed initiatives
   - 🟡 Yellow: Paused initiatives
4. Click any marker to see initiative details
5. Click "View Details" in popup to go to full page

### Viewing Initiative Details

Click on any initiative card or map marker to see full details:

**Information Displayed**:
- Full title and description
- Status and forest badges
- Progress section with:
  - Progress bar showing completion percentage
  - Trees planted vs. target
  - Trees remaining
  - Days remaining (if end date set)
  - On-track indicator (green ✓ or red ⚠)
- Details section:
  - Timeline (start and end dates)
  - Area in hectares
  - Location coordinates
  - Number of participants
- Participant list (top 10 shown)
- Join button (for non-participants)

### Joining an Initiative

**For Community Members and Individuals**:

1. Browse initiatives or view on map
2. Click on an initiative to view details
3. Click the **"Join Initiative"** button
4. You'll see a confirmation message
5. Your name appears in the participant list
6. You can now contribute trees to this initiative

**Note**: You can only join active initiatives. Completed or paused initiatives cannot be joined.

### Tracking Your Contributions

**After Joining**:

1. View the initiative details page
2. Find the "Your Contribution" section
3. Click "Update Contribution"
4. Enter the number of trees you've planted
5. Click "Save"
6. Your contribution is updated in real-time

**Contribution Tracker Features**:
- Display mode showing current contribution
- Edit mode with form for updating
- Validation and error handling
- Success feedback
- Large, clear number display

## Tree Registry & Monitoring

### What is the Tree Registry?

The tree registry is a comprehensive system for tracking individual trees, monitoring their health, and documenting their growth over time with AI-powered analysis.

**Status**: ✅ FULLY OPERATIONAL

### Registering a Tree

**Step-by-Step Guide**:

1. **Navigate to Tree Registry**
   - Go to your dashboard
   - Click "Register a Tree" button

2. **Fill in Tree Information**
   - **Species**: Select from dropdown or enter custom species
     - Indigenous species are highlighted
     - Example: "Acacia mearnsii", "Croton megalocarpus"
   - **Planting Date**: When the tree was planted
     - Required field
   - **Location**: Click on map or enter coordinates
     - Use preset buttons for forest locations
     - Or click anywhere on the map
   - **Forest**: Select forest (Kakamega, Karura, or Mau)
   - **Initiative** (Optional): Link to an initiative
   - **Current Height** (Optional): Height in meters
   - **Notes** (Optional): Any additional information

3. **Review and Submit**
   - Check all information is correct
   - Click "Register Tree"
   - Wait for confirmation
   - You'll be redirected to the tree details page

### Uploading Tree Photos

**Why Upload Photos?**
- Track growth visually over time
- Enable AI-powered health analysis
- Document tree development
- Share progress with the community

**How to Upload**:

1. **Navigate to Tree Details**
   - Go to "My Trees" or "Tree Registry"
   - Click on a tree to view details
   - Find the "Upload Photo" section

2. **Upload Image**
   - **Option A: Drag and Drop**
     - Drag image file into the upload area
   - **Option B: Click to Browse**
     - Click "Choose File"
     - Select image from your device

3. **Add Photo Details**
   - **Capture Date**: When the photo was taken
     - Defaults to today
   - **Notes** (Optional): Observations about the tree
     - Example: "First month growth", "After pruning"

4. **Submit**
   - Click "Upload"
   - Wait for upload to complete
   - Photo appears in the gallery

**Photo Requirements**:
- **Formats**: JPEG, PNG, WebP
- **Maximum Size**: 10MB
- **Recommended**: Clear, well-lit photos showing the full tree

### Viewing Tree Details

**Information Displayed**:

1. **Basic Information**
   - Species name
   - Planting date
   - Age (calculated automatically)
   - Forest location
   - Initiative (if linked)
   - Planter name

2. **Health Status**
   - Visual health indicator (color-coded)
   - Health score (0-100)
   - Status: Healthy, At Risk, or Deceased
   - Last updated timestamp

3. **Growth Tracking**
   - Current height
   - Growth chart (height over time)
   - Growth trend indicator
   - Interactive data points

4. **Photo Gallery**
   - Grid of all uploaded photos
   - Click to view full-size
   - Navigate between photos
   - Capture dates displayed
   - Delete option (for your photos)

5. **AI Analysis Results** (if available)
   - Health assessment
   - Disease detection
   - Pest detection
   - Stress indicators
   - Care recommendations
   - Confidence scores

### AI-Powered Health Analysis

**How It Works**:

1. **Automatic Analysis**
   - When you upload a photo, it's automatically sent to Antugrow AI
   - Analysis typically completes within 24 hours
   - You'll receive a notification when results are ready

2. **What's Analyzed**:
   - **Health Score**: Overall health rating (0-100)
   - **Diseases**: Detection of common tree diseases
   - **Pests**: Identification of pest infestations
   - **Stress Indicators**: Water stress, nutrient deficiency, etc.
   - **Growth Rate**: Comparison to expected growth
   - **Predictions**: Future growth projections

3. **Care Recommendations**:
   - Actionable suggestions for tree care
   - Watering schedules
   - Fertilization recommendations
   - Pest control measures
   - Pruning guidance

**Viewing Analysis Results**:

1. Go to tree details page
2. Scroll to "AI Analysis" section
3. View latest analysis results
4. Click "View History" to see past analyses
5. Follow care recommendations

- **Initiative**: Filter by specific initiative

## Creating an Initiative (Organizations Only)

### Step-by-Step Guide

**Prerequisites**:
- You must have an "Organization" role
- You must be logged in

**Steps**:

1. **Navigate to Create Initiative**
   - Go to your dashboard
   - Click "Create Initiative" button

2. **Fill in Basic Information**
   - **Title**: Give your initiative a descriptive name
     - Example: "Kakamega Forest Restoration 2025"
   - **Description**: Explain the goals and activities
     - Be specific about what you plan to do
   - **Target Forest**: Select from dropdown
     - Kakamega, Karura, or Mau

3. **Set Targets and Timeline**
   - **Target Trees**: Number of trees you plan to plant
     - Must be a positive number
   - **Area**: Size of the planting area in hectares
     - Must be a positive number
   - **Start Date**: When the initiative begins
     - Required field
   - **End Date**: When the initiative ends (optional)
     - Must be after start date

4. **Select Location on Map**
   - Use preset buttons for general forest areas
   - Click on the map for specific locations
   - Or enter coordinates manually
   - The marker shows your selected location

5. **Review and Submit**
   - Check all information is correct
   - Click "Create Initiative"
   - Wait for confirmation
   - You'll be redirected to the initiative details page

### Tips for Creating Initiatives

**Title**:
- Keep it under 200 characters
- Include the forest name and year
- Make it descriptive and engaging

**Description**:
- Explain the purpose and goals
- Mention the tree species (if known)
- Include any special activities or events
- Describe the expected impact

**Location**:
- Be as accurate as possible
- Use the preset buttons for general forest areas
- Click on the map for specific locations
- Double-check coordinates before submitting

**Targets**:
- Set realistic tree planting goals
- Consider the area size and timeline
- Account for seasonal factors
- Plan for community participation

## FAQ

### Account & Registration

**Q: Why do I need to complete my profile?**  
A: Your profile helps us personalize your experience and connect you with relevant conservation opportunities. Some features require a complete profile.

**Q: Can I change my role later?**  
A: Yes! You can update your role in your profile settings at any time.

**Q: Is my information secure?**  
A: Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and security practices. Your data is stored securely and never shared without your permission.

### Initiatives

**Q: How do I find initiatives near me?**  
A: Use the map view to see initiatives geographically. You can also filter by forest to see initiatives in specific areas.

**Q: Can I join multiple initiatives?**  
A: Yes! You can join as many initiatives as you want. There's no limit.

**Q: What happens when I join an initiative?**  
A: You become a participant and your name appears in the participant list. You can track your contributions and receive updates about the initiative.

**Q: Can I leave an initiative after joining?**  
A: Yes, you can leave an initiative at any time. Your contributions will still be recorded.

**Q: How is progress tracked?**  
A: Progress is calculated based on trees planted vs. target trees. The system also tracks if an initiative is on track based on time elapsed and progress made.

### Tree Registry

**Q: Who can register trees?**  
A: Community members and organizations can register trees. Individuals can view the registry but cannot register trees yet.

**Q: Can I register trees I planted before joining the platform?**  
A: Yes! You can register any tree you've planted, regardless of when it was planted. Just enter the correct planting date.

**Q: How many photos can I upload per tree?**  
A: There's no limit! We encourage you to upload photos regularly to track growth over time.

**Q: How long does AI analysis take?**  
A: AI analysis typically completes within 24 hours of uploading a photo. You'll receive a notification when results are ready.

**Q: What if the AI detects a problem with my tree?**  
A: The AI will provide care recommendations. Follow these suggestions and monitor your tree closely. If problems persist, consult a local forestry expert.

**Q: Can I link a tree to multiple initiatives?**  
A: No, each tree can only be linked to one initiative. However, you can register multiple trees for different initiatives.

**Q: Can I delete a tree registration?**  
A: Yes, you can delete trees you've registered. However, this action is permanent and cannot be undone.

### Maps

**Q: Why can't I see the map?**  
A: Make sure you have a stable internet connection. The maps require loading map tiles from OpenStreetMap. If the problem persists, try refreshing the page.

**Q: How accurate are the forest boundaries?**  
A: The forest boundaries shown are approximate and simplified for visualization purposes. They provide a general idea of forest locations and sizes.

**Q: Can I use the map on mobile?**  
A: Yes! The maps are mobile-friendly and support touch gestures for panning and zooming.

### Photos & Images

**Q: What photo format should I use?**  
A: We accept JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats. JPEG is recommended for best compatibility.

**Q: Why is my photo upload failing?**  
A: Check that your photo is under 10MB and in a supported format (JPEG, PNG, WebP). Also ensure you have a stable internet connection.

**Q: Can I edit photos after uploading?**  
A: You cannot edit photos after uploading, but you can delete them and upload new ones.

### Technical Issues

**Q: I didn't receive a verification email**  
A: Check your spam folder. If still not found, click "Resend verification email" on the login page.

**Q: I forgot my password**  
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions to reset it.

**Q: The map is not loading**  
A: Check your internet connection. Try refreshing the page. If the problem persists, try clearing your browser cache.

**Q: My tree photos aren't showing up**  
A: Photos may take a few moments to process. Refresh the page after 30 seconds. If they still don't appear, try uploading again.
