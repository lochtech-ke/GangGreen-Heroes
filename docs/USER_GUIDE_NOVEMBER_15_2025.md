## Tree Planting Initiatives

### What Are Initiatives?

Tree planting initiatives are organized conservation projects where organizations coordinate efforts to plant trees and restore forests.

**Status**: ✅ FULLY OPERATIONAL

### Browsing Initiatives

#### List View

1. Navigate to "Initiatives" from the main menu
2. See all active initiatives in a grid layout
3. Each card shows:
   - Title and description
   - Forest location
   - Status (Active, Completed, Paused)
   - Progress bar with percentage
   - Trees planted vs. target
   - Area and timeline

#### Filtering

Use filters to find specific initiatives:
- **Forest**: Kakamega, Karura, or Mau
- **Status**: Active, Completed, or Paused
- **Search**: Search by title or description

#### Map View

1. Click "View on Map" to see initiatives geographically
2. Color-coded markers show status:
   - 🟢 Green: Active
   - 🔵 Blue: Completed
   - 🟡 Yellow: Paused
3. Click markers for details

## Joining an Initiative

### How to Join

**For Community Members and Individuals**:

1. Browse initiatives or view on map
2. Click on an initiative to view details
3. Click the **"Join Initiative"** button
4. You'll see a confirmation
5. Your name appears in the participant list

**Requirements**:
- Must have a user account
- Initiative must be active
- You can only join once per initiative

### What Happens When You Join

- ✅ You become a participant
- ✅ Your name appears in the participant list
- ✅ You can track your contributions
- ✅ You'll see milestone celebrations
- ✅ You can update your tree count

### Leaving an Initiative

If you need to leave:

1. Go to the initiative details page
2. Click "Leave Initiative" (below the Participating badge)
3. Confirm your decision
4. Your participation record will be removed

**Note**: This action cannot be undone. You can rejoin later if needed.

## Tracking Your Contributions

### Your Contribution Tracker

Once you've joined an initiative, you'll see your contribution tracker:

**Display Mode**:
- Shows your current tree count in large numbers
- Click "Update" to change your contribution

**Update Mode**:
1. Click the "Update" button
2. Enter the total number of trees you've planted
3. Click "Save Contribution"
4. Success message appears
5. Initiative progress updates automatically

### Tips for Tracking

- **Be Accurate**: Enter the actual number of trees planted
- **Update Regularly**: Keep your count current
- **Total Count**: Enter your total, not just new trees
- **Validation**: The system won't accept negative numbers

### Example

```
Current Contribution: 25 trees
↓
Click "Update"
↓
Enter new total: 50 trees
↓
Click "Save Contribution"
↓
Success! Your contribution: 50 trees
```

## Understanding Milestones

### What Are Milestones?

Milestones celebrate progress toward the initiative's goal. They occur at:

- **25%**: 🌱 Quarter way there!
- **50%**: 🌳 Halfway to the goal!
- **75%**: 🌲 Three quarters complete!
- **90%**: 🎯 Almost there!
- **100%**: 🎉 Goal achieved!

### Milestone Celebrations

When a milestone is reached:

1. **Animated Alert** appears at the top
   - Shows the milestone percentage
   - Displays celebration message
   - Auto-dismisses after 5 seconds

2. **Milestone Card** updates
   - Changes from gray to colored
   - Shows completion checkmark
   - Displays achievement message

3. **Progress Indicator** shows next milestone
   - "X more trees to go!"
   - Encourages continued participation

### Milestone Colors

- 25%: Green gradient 🟢
- 50%: Blue gradient 🔵
- 75%: Purple gradient 🟣
- 90%: Orange gradient 🟠
- 100%: Yellow gradient 🟡

## FAQ

### Joining Initiatives

**Q: Can I join multiple initiatives?**  
A: Yes! You can join as many initiatives as you want.

**Q: Can I join a completed initiative?**  
A: No, you can only join active initiatives. Completed and paused initiatives don't accept new participants.

**Q: What if I accidentally join?**  
A: You can leave at any time by clicking "Leave Initiative" and confirming.

### Tracking Contributions

**Q: How do I update my tree count?**  
A: Click the "Update" button in your contribution tracker, enter your total trees planted, and click "Save Contribution".

**Q: Can I decrease my contribution?**  
A: Yes, you can update to any non-negative number. Enter your current accurate total.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**  
A: Simply update your contribution again with the correct number.

**Q: Do my contributions affect the initiative progress?**  
A: Yes! When you update your contribution, the initiative's overall progress recalculates automatically.

### Milestones

**Q: Who sees milestone celebrations?**  
A: Everyone viewing the initiative details page sees milestone progress and celebrations.

**Q: Can I trigger a milestone?**  
A: Yes! If your contribution update pushes the initiative past a milestone threshold, the celebration will display.

**Q: What happens at 100%?**  
A: The initiative is marked as completed, and a special celebration displays with confetti emoji 🎉.

### Participants

**Q: Can I see who else joined?**  
A: Yes, the participant list shows all participants with their join dates and contributions.

**Q: Is my contribution visible to others?**  
A: Yes, your tree contribution count is visible in the participant list for transparency.

**Q: Can I hide my participation?**  
A: No, participation is public to encourage transparency and community engagement.

### Leaving Initiatives

**Q: What happens when I leave?**  
A: Your participation record is removed, including your contribution count. You won't appear in the participant list.

**Q: Can I rejoin after leaving?**  
A: Yes, you can rejoin at any time if the initiative is still active.

**Q: Will my trees still count?**  
A: No, when you leave, your contribution is removed from the initiative's total.
