# Tax Receipt Policy

**Last Updated: November 18, 2025**

## 1. Overview

This Tax Receipt Policy explains how #GangGreen platform users can obtain tax receipts for eligible donations and the requirements for claiming tax deductions in Kenya.

## 2. Eligibility for Tax Receipts

### 2.1 Qualifying Donations

Tax receipts are issued for:
- Monetary donations to KRA-approved charitable organizations
- Tree planting contributions to registered conservation initiatives
- Carbon credit purchases supporting verified conservation projects
- Recurring donations made through the Platform

### 2.2 Non-Qualifying Transactions

Tax receipts are NOT issued for:
- NFT badge purchases (considered digital collectibles)
- GG Coin purchases (virtual currency with no monetary value)
- Marketplace fees or transaction charges
- Cryptocurrency donations (subject to different tax treatment)

## 3. Kenyan Tax Deduction Framework

### 3.1 Legal Basis

Under Section 15(2)(p) of the Kenya Income Tax Act:
- Donations to approved charitable organizations are tax-deductible
- Organizations must have valid KRA tax exemption certificates
- Deductions are subject to limits and conditions

### 3.2 Deduction Limits

**Individual Taxpayers:**
- Maximum deduction typically capped at percentage of taxable income
- Specific limits vary based on current KRA regulations
- Consult KRA guidelines or tax professional for current limits

**Corporate Taxpayers:**
- Different limits may apply for corporate donations
- Must comply with corporate tax regulations

### 3.3 Approved Organizations

Tax deductions are only available for donations to:
- Organizations registered with KRA as charitable entities
- Organizations with valid tax exemption certificates
- Organizations operating for charitable purposes including:
  - Environmental conservation
  - Poverty alleviation
  - Education and health
  - Community development

## 4. Tax Receipt Issuance

### 4.1 Automatic Receipts

**Immediate Issuance:**
- Tax receipts automatically generated for qualifying donations
- Sent via email within 24 hours of donation
- Available for download in user dashboard

**Receipt Contents:**
- Donor name and KRA PIN (if provided)
- Donation amount and date
- Recipient organization details
- Organization's KRA PIN and tax exemption certificate number
- Receipt number for tracking
- Platform transaction ID

### 4.2 Manual Requests

If you don't receive an automatic receipt:
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Profile > Donations > Tax Receipts
3. Select the donation and click "Request Receipt"
4. Receipt will be emailed within 3 business days

### 4.3 Amended Receipts

To request corrections:
- Email: tax-receipts@ganggreen.africa
- Include: Original receipt number, donation date, correction needed
- Amended receipts issued within 5 business days

## 5. Annual Tax Summaries

### 5.1 Year-End Reports

**Automatic Generation:**
- Annual donation summaries generated each January
- Covers all donations from previous calendar year (January 1 - December 31)
- Emailed to all donors who made qualifying donations

**Summary Contents:**
- Total donations by organization
- Total donations by month
- Breakdown by donation type
- Estimated tax deduction amount (informational only)
- List of all individual receipts

### 5.2 Accessing Summaries

**Dashboard Access:**
1. Profile > Tax Documents > Annual Summaries
2. Select year
3. Download PDF or CSV format

**Custom Date Ranges:**
- Generate reports for custom periods
- Useful for mid-year tax planning
- Available in dashboard

## 6. KRA PIN Requirements

### 6.1 Providing Your KRA PIN

**Optional but Recommended:**
- KRA PIN not required for donations
- Providing PIN enables proper tax receipt generation
- PIN stored securely and used only for tax documentation

**How to Add:**
1. Profile > Settings > Tax Information
2. Enter KRA PIN
3. Verify with KRA (optional verification)

### 6.2 Privacy and Security

- KRA PINs encrypted in our database
- Used only for tax receipt purposes
- Never shared with third parties except as required by law
- Can be removed at any time

## 7. Documentation Requirements

### 7.1 For Tax Filing

When claiming deductions, maintain:
- Official tax receipts from #GangGreen platform
- Annual donation summaries
- Bank statements or payment confirmations
- Organization's tax exemption certificate (available on request)

### 7.2 Record Retention

**Recommended Period:**
- Keep tax receipts for at least 7 years
- KRA may request documentation during audits
- Digital copies acceptable

**Platform Storage:**
- We retain receipt records for 7 years
- Accessible through your account
- Available even after account closure (upon request)

## 8. Cryptocurrency Donations

### 8.1 Special Tax Treatment

**Important Notice:**
- Cryptocurrency donations have different tax implications
- May be treated as property disposal rather than charitable donation
- Capital gains tax may apply
- Tax deduction eligibility depends on specific circumstances

### 8.2 Cryptocurrency Receipts

We provide:
- Transaction confirmation with blockchain hash
- Donation amount in cryptocurrency and KES equivalent
- Date and time of transaction
- Recipient wallet address

**Tax Advice Required:**
- Consult tax professional for cryptocurrency donation treatment
- KRA guidance on cryptocurrency taxation is evolving
