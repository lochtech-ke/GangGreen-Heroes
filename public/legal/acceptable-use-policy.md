# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last Updated: November 18, 2025**

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of the #GangGreen platform. By accessing or using the Platform, you agree to comply with this policy. Violations may result in account suspension or termination.

## 2. Permitted Uses

The Platform is designed for:
- Participating in environmental conservation initiatives
- Making donations to support tree planting and forest conservation
- Trading verified carbon credits
- Engaging with the community around sustainability
- Tracking environmental impact
- Earning and displaying NFT badges for conservation achievements
- Connecting Web3 wallets for cryptocurrency donations
- Sharing conservation success stories and updates

## 3. Prohibited Activities

### 3.1 Illegal Activities

You may not use the Platform to:
- Violate any local, national, or international law
- Engage in money laundering or terrorist financing
- Facilitate illegal trade or transactions
- Infringe on intellectual property rights
- Violate export control or sanctions laws
- Engage in fraud or misrepresentation

### 3.2 Harmful Content

You may not post, upload, or share content that:
- Contains hate speech, discrimination, or harassment
- Promotes violence or terrorism
- Is sexually explicit or pornographic
- Exploits or harms minors
- Contains graphic violence or gore
- Promotes self-harm or suicide
- Is defamatory or libelous

### 3.3 Fraudulent Activities

You may not:
- Create fake accounts or impersonate others
- Misrepresent your identity or affiliation
- Provide false information about initiatives or donations
- Manipulate carbon credit verification processes
- Engage in phishing or social engineering
- Create fraudulent conservation initiatives
- Falsify tree planting data or impact metrics

### 3.4 Platform Abuse

You may not:
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Platform
- Interfere with Platform operations or security
- Reverse engineer or decompile Platform code
- Use automated tools (bots, scrapers) without permission
- Overload or disrupt Platform infrastructure
- Bypass security measures or access controls
- Exploit bugs or vulnerabilities for personal gain

### 3.5 Gamification Manipulation

You may not:
- Use exploits to gain unfair advantages in gamification systems
- Create multiple accounts to farm points or rewards
- Manipulate leaderboards or achievement systems
- Trade or sell GG Coins (they have no monetary value)
- Automate actions to earn points artificially

### 3.6 Market Manipulation

You may not:
- Engage in wash trading of carbon credits
- Artificially inflate or deflate carbon credit prices
- Coordinate market manipulation schemes
- Provide misleading information about carbon credits
- Front-run transactions or engage in insider trading

### 3.7 Spam and Unwanted Communications

You may not:
- Send unsolicited commercial messages
- Post repetitive or irrelevant content
- Engage in excessive self-promotion
- Distribute malware or malicious links
- Harvest user data for marketing purposes

### 3.8 Intellectual Property Violations

You may not:
- Upload content you don't have rights to use
- Infringe on copyrights, trademarks, or patents
- Use Platform branding without authorization
- Copy or redistribute Platform content without permission
- Create derivative works without authorization

## 4. User-Generated Content

### 4.1 Content Standards

When posting content, you must:
- Ensure accuracy of information
- Respect others' privacy and rights
- Use appropriate language and tone
- Stay relevant to conservation and sustainability
- Provide proper attribution for others' work

### 4.2 Content Ownership

- You retain ownership of content you post
- You grant us license to use your content (see Terms of Service)
- You are responsible for your content
- We may remove content that violates this policy

### 4.3 Prohibited Content Types

Do not post:
- Misleading conservation claims
- Unverified environmental data
- Private information about others
- Confidential or proprietary information
- Content that violates third-party rights

## 5. Initiative and Organization Standards

### 5.1 Initiative Creation

Organizations creating initiatives must:
- Provide accurate information about conservation projects
- Have legitimate authority to conduct initiatives
- Comply with local environmental regulations
- Maintain transparency in fund usage
- Provide regular updates on progress
- Verify tree planting and impact data

### 5.2 Prohibited Initiative Practices

Organizations may not:
- Create fake or fraudulent initiatives
- Misappropriate donated funds
- Exaggerate environmental impact
- Fail to deliver on promised activities
- Discriminate in participant selection
- Violate environmental protection laws

## 6. Carbon Credit Standards

### 6.1 Seller Requirements

Carbon credit sellers must:
- Provide accurate credit information
- Ensure credits are properly verified
- Not sell the same credit multiple times (double-counting)
- Comply with verification standards
- Maintain proper documentation
- Disclose any conflicts of interest

### 6.2 Buyer Responsibilities

Carbon credit buyers must:
- Conduct due diligence on credits
- Use credits for legitimate offset purposes
- Not engage in fraudulent claims
- Comply with reporting requirements

## 7. Web3 and Cryptocurrency

### 7.1 Wallet Connections

When connecting wallets, you must:
- Use wallets you legitimately control
- Protect your private keys and seed phrases
- Not share wallet access credentials
- Comply with cryptocurrency regulations in your jurisdiction

### 7.2 Cryptocurrency Donations

You may not:
- Use cryptocurrency from illegal sources
- Donate to evade taxes or regulations
- Engage in money laundering through donations
- Use mixer or tumbler services to obscure transaction origins

### 7.3 NFT Badges

You may not:
- Create fake or counterfeit NFT badges
- Manipulate badge rarity or attributes
- Use badges for fraudulent purposes
- Misrepresent badge ownership or value

## 8. Privacy and Data Protection

### 8.1 Respecting Privacy

You must:
- Respect other users' privacy
- Not collect or harvest user data
- Not share others' personal information
- Comply with data protection laws

### 8.2 Your Data

You are responsible for:
- Protecting your account credentials
- Maintaining accurate profile information
- Managing your privacy settings
- Reporting security concerns

## 9. Community Conduct

### 9.1 Respectful Interaction

You must:
- Treat others with respect and courtesy
- Engage in constructive dialogue
- Respect diverse perspectives
- Report violations appropriately

### 9.2 Prohibited Behavior

You may not:
- Harass, bully, or threaten others
- Engage in discriminatory behavior
- Dox or reveal others' private information
- Coordinate harassment campaigns
- Create hostile environments

## 10. Commercial Activities

### 10.1 Permitted Commercial Use

Authorized commercial activities:
- Selling verified carbon credits through marketplace
- Organizational fundraising for conservation
- Sponsored conservation initiatives (with disclosure)

### 10.2 Prohibited Commercial Use

You may not:
- Use Platform for unrelated commercial purposes
- Advertise non-conservation products or services
- Engage in multi-level marketing or pyramid schemes
- Solicit users for external business
- Use Platform data for commercial purposes without permission

## 11. Reporting Violations

### 11.1 How to Report

If you observe violations:
1. Use in-platform reporting tools
2. Email: abuse@ganggreen.africa
3. Provide specific details and evidence
4. Include relevant usernames, links, or screenshots

### 11.2 What to Report

Report:
- Fraudulent initiatives or activities
- Abusive or harmful content
- Security vulnerabilities
- Spam or unwanted communications
- Intellectual property violations
- Any violation of this policy

### 11.3 False Reports

Do not:
- File false or malicious reports
- Abuse reporting systems
- Retaliate against users who report violations

## 12. Enforcement

### 12.1 Investigation

We will:
- Investigate reported violations
- Review evidence and context
- Contact involved parties when appropriate
- Make determinations based on policy and evidence

### 12.2 Consequences

Violations may result in:

- Reporting to law enforcement

### 12.3 Appeals

1. Email: appeals@ganggreen.africa
2. Provide account details and explanation
3. Include any relevant evidence
4. Appeals reviewed within 10 business days
