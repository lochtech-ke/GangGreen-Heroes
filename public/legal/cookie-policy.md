# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated: November 18, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Loch Tech Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on the #GangGreen platform ("Platform"). This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience.

### 2.1 Types of Cookies by Duration

**Session Cookies:** Temporary cookies deleted when you close your browser

**Persistent Cookies:** Remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted

### 2.2 Types of Cookies by Origin

**First-Party Cookies:** Set by the #GangGreen Platform

**Third-Party Cookies:** Set by external services we use (e.g., Google Analytics, Mapbox)

## 4. Similar Technologies

### 4.1 Local Storage

We use browser local storage to:
- Cache user preferences
- Store draft content
- Improve performance
- Remember wallet connections

### 4.2 Session Storage

Temporary storage cleared when you close your browser:
- Form data during multi-step processes
- Temporary authentication tokens

### 4.3 Web Beacons (Pixels)

Small transparent images used to:
- Track email opens
- Measure campaign effectiveness
- Analyze user behavior

### 4.4 Blockchain Tracking

Wallet addresses and transactions are publicly visible on blockchain networks. This is inherent to blockchain technology and not controlled by cookies.

## 5. How to Manage Cookies

### 5.1 Platform Cookie Settings

Control cookies through our Platform:
1. Go to Settings > Privacy > Cookie Preferences
2. Toggle cookie categories on/off
3. Save your preferences

Note: Essential cookies cannot be disabled.

### 5.2 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to:
- Block all cookies
- Block third-party cookies only
- Delete cookies after each session
- Receive notifications before cookies are set

**Browser-Specific Instructions:**

**Google Chrome:**
Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data

**Mozilla Firefox:**
Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data

**Safari:**
Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data

**Microsoft Edge:**
Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

**Mobile Browsers:**
Check your device settings or browser app settings

### 5.3 Third-Party Opt-Out Tools

**Google Analytics Opt-out:**  
https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

**Network Advertising Initiative:**  
https://optout.networkadvertising.org/

**Digital Advertising Alliance:**  
https://optout.aboutads.info/

### 5.4 Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers offer "Do Not Track" signals. Currently, there is no industry standard for responding to DNT signals. We do not alter our data collection practices in response to DNT signals.

## 6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Disabling cookies may affect your experience:

**Essential Cookies Disabled:**
- Cannot log in or maintain session
- Security features may not work
- Platform may be unusable

**Analytics Cookies Disabled:**
- No impact on functionality
- Helps us improve the Platform less effectively

**Preference Cookies Disabled:**
- Settings not remembered between sessions
- Must reconfigure preferences each visit

**Functional Cookies Disabled:**
- Maps may not load properly
- Wallet connections may not persist
- Some features may be limited
